## Mod Introduction
This is a difficulty adjustment mod that increases game difficulty by giving Heroes and enemies various effects through Torches, similar to the Farmstead.  
Additionally, this mod changes how Light Levels affect difficulty, giving high and low Light Levels their own risks and rewards.  
(※ Artwork and audio uses original game assets and AI-generated content, which have been further edited.)

## Enemy Enhancements
Provides general enhancements to enemies in all regions, while enemies in different regions also receive their own regional enhancements.  

## Torch Light Level Changes
Light is now divided into 6 levels. High and low Light Levels increase difficulty in different ways while also increasing drop rates. Medium Light has no effect.  
Light changes randomly at the start of the first combat of a quest.  
(※If you exit and re-enter the game while in a quest, the Light will be randomized again at the start of the next round.)

## Flame Effects
During the first combat of a quest, Torches have a chance to grant random Flame Effects. These effects last for the entire quest and cannot be removed by Medicinal Herbs. The effects granted are affected by Party size.  
(※If you exit and re-enter the game while in a quest, the existing Flame Effects are cleared at the start of the next round, and new effects are randomized.)

## New Enemy
### Lamp Keeper
Added a new enemy, the "Lamp Keeper". She can be encountered in the Ruins, Cove, Warrens, and Weald, usually in position 4.  
She has two actions per round, supporting her Party from the back ranks or inflicting Stress damage on all Heroes.
When in position 4, she gains additional Dodge and remain in Stealth. If she is moved out of position 4, her Dodge is reduced and she will De-stealth. Quickly eliminating one of her allies can make the "Lamp Keeper" easier to deal with.  
Defeating she grants Gems, Trinkets, and mod items, as well as a chance to obtain Trinkets dropped exclusively by her.  
![image](https://file.vidhubfile.com/imgtok/post/2HU6a7U/f_003.png)

## New Boss
### Abyssal Shambler
Adds an enhanced version of the Shambler, which can be encountered by using a mod item.  
Defeating it grants new Quirks, a new gem, and new Trinkets. Champion Level and above encounters can also reward more powerful Trinkets.  
(※Since it uses the same animation files as the original Shambler, Shambler skin mods should theoretically also work on the Abyssal Shambler.)

## Compatibility
This mod works in the Courtyard and Farmstead, with dedicated regional enhancements for both areas.  
This mod includes basic compatibility adjustments for the following Dungeon mods:  
Courtyard Plus, Farmstead Plus, Pet Cemetery, The Fiend Festival, Sunward Isles, Warpstone Mines.  

This mod should also work with new Dungeon mods that do not use special Torch settings.  
The following mods have been confirmed not to use special Torch settings (2026/10/12):  
The Mountain, The Manor, Town Wars Rework (2020)
